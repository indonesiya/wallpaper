Being used in a relationship can be emotionally draining and difficult to recognize. Sometimes, affection and kindness can hide selfish intentions. Over time, however, patterns of behavior often reveal the truth.

A healthy relationship is based on mutual support and fairness. When one partner consistently benefits while the other sacrifices, the relationship becomes unbalanced.

Understanding the signs can help you protect your emotional and financial well-being.

## Common Signs Your Partner May Be Using You

### They Ask for Help Frequently

Your partner may rely on you for money, favors, or emotional support without giving back.

This imbalance creates frustration.

### They Disappear When You Need Support

They may be unavailable during your difficult moments.

This behavior shows lack of reciprocity.

### Lack of Appreciation

Your efforts may go unnoticed.

Gratitude strengthens relationships.

### Conditional Affection

They show kindness only when they want something.

This pattern signals manipulation.

## How to Protect Yourself

- Recognize unhealthy patterns
- Set boundaries
- Stop overgiving
- Communicate your expectations
- Evaluate the relationship honestly
Healthy relationships involve balance and respect.

You deserve support as much as you give it.
