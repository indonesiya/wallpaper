Commitment is essential for building a strong and lasting relationship. When your partner is not fully committed, it can create uncertainty and emotional instability.

This lack of commitment often appears through inconsistent behavior and unclear intentions.

Recognizing the signs can help you make informed decisions.

## Avoiding Long-Term Plans

Your partner may avoid discussions about the future.

They prefer to stay focused on the present.

This can indicate hesitation.

## Inconsistent Effort

Their level of effort may change frequently.

This inconsistency can be confusing.

It reflects uncertainty.

## Keeping Options Open

Your partner may act as if they are not fully invested.

This can make you feel like an option.

This is a red flag.

## Lack of Emotional Investment

They may not fully engage emotionally.

This creates distance.

Connection weakens.

## Emotional Impact

Being in a non-committed relationship can lead to anxiety and insecurity.

You may feel unsure about your future.

This affects your well-being.

## How to Address It

Seek clarity through honest conversation.

- Ask about their intentions
- Express your expectations
- Observe consistency
Actions matter most.

## Knowing When to Walk Away

If commitment is not mutual, it may be time to reconsider the relationship.

You deserve certainty and stability.

Do not settle for less.

## Conclusion

Commitment is key to a successful relationship.

By recognizing the signs, you can protect your emotional well-being.

Healthy relationships require mutual dedication.

Choose clarity over confusion.
