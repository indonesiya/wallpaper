In a healthy relationship, both partners should feel like a priority. If you feel like an option, it can create insecurity and emotional confusion.

This dynamic often involves inconsistency and lack of commitment.

Recognizing the signs can help you protect your self-worth.

## Inconsistent Attention

Your partner may give attention only when it suits them.

This creates uncertainty.

Consistency is important.

## Avoiding Commitment

They may avoid defining the relationship.

This can leave you feeling unsure.

Clarity is essential.

## Prioritizing Others

Your partner may place others ahead of you.

This shows where you stand.

It reflects imbalance.

## Mixed Signals

Their actions may not match their words.

This creates confusion.

Actions reveal intentions.

## Emotional Impact

Being treated as an option can harm your confidence.

You may feel undervalued.

This affects your well-being.

## How to Respond

Take control of the situation.

- Ask for clarity
- Set boundaries
- Know your worth
You deserve certainty.

## When to Walk Away

If your partner cannot prioritize you, it may be time to move on.

Do not settle for less.

Choose respect.

## Conclusion

A healthy relationship involves mutual priority and respect.

By recognizing the signs, you can make empowered decisions.

You deserve to be chosen, not considered an option.

Value yourself.
