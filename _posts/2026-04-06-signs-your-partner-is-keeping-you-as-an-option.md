Feeling like a backup plan in a relationship can be deeply hurtful. When a partner keeps you as an option, they may show interest only when it is convenient for them. This behavior creates uncertainty and emotional instability.

Healthy relationships involve commitment and consistent effort. If you feel unsure about your partner's intentions, it may be time to evaluate the situation honestly.

Recognizing the signs early can protect your self-respect.

## Common Signs You Are Being Kept as an Option

### Inconsistent Communication

Your partner contacts you only when they need something.

This pattern shows lack of commitment.

### Avoiding Commitment

They hesitate to define the relationship.

This hesitation creates uncertainty.

### Last-Minute Plans

You may be invited only when their schedule is free.

This behavior indicates low priority.

### Lack of Long-Term Planning

Your partner avoids discussing the future.

This avoidance signals emotional distance.

## What You Can Do

- Clarify your expectations
- Observe consistent behavior
- Set clear boundaries
- Value your self-worth
- Be prepared to walk away if necessary
You deserve a relationship where you are a priority, not an option.

Respect and commitment are essential for emotional security.
