Feeling unappreciated in a relationship can be emotionally painful. When a partner begins to take you for granted, your efforts may go unnoticed, and your needs may be ignored. Over time, this pattern can lead to frustration and resentment.

Healthy relationships involve mutual appreciation and respect. Both partners should recognize each other's contributions and show gratitude regularly.

Recognizing the warning signs early can help prevent emotional burnout.

## What Does It Mean to Be Taken for Granted?

Being taken for granted means your partner expects your support without acknowledging your efforts. They may assume you will always be there, regardless of how they behave.

This attitude creates imbalance in the relationship.

## Common Signs Your Partner Is Taking You for Granted

### 1. Lack of Appreciation

Your partner rarely says thank you or recognizes your efforts.

This absence of gratitude can feel discouraging.

### 2. Minimal Effort

They stop planning activities or showing affection.

The relationship becomes routine.

### 3. Ignoring Your Needs

Your concerns may be dismissed or overlooked.

This behavior creates emotional distance.

### 4. Assuming You Will Always Stay

Your partner may believe you will never leave.

This assumption can lead to complacency.

## How to Respond When You Feel Taken for Granted

- Communicate your feelings clearly
- Set healthy boundaries
- Stop overgiving
- Focus on self-respect
- Evaluate the relationship honestly
Appreciation is essential for maintaining emotional connection.

A relationship should make you feel valued, not invisible.
