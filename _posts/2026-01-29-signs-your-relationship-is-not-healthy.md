A healthy relationship should provide support, respect, and emotional safety. When these elements are missing, the relationship may become unhealthy.

Recognizing the signs early can help you take action and protect your well-being.

Awareness is key to making better decisions.

## Lack of Respect

Respect is fundamental in any relationship.

If your partner dismisses your feelings or boundaries, it is a warning sign.

This creates imbalance.

## Poor Communication

Communication issues can lead to misunderstandings.

Frequent arguments or silence can indicate deeper problems.

This weakens the connection.

## Control or Manipulation

Controlling behavior limits your independence.

Manipulation can make you doubt yourself.

This is unhealthy.

## Emotional or Physical Harm

Any form of harm is a serious issue.

This includes emotional abuse or physical violence.

Safety should never be compromised.

## Feeling Unhappy

You may feel more unhappy than happy.

This is a strong indicator that something is wrong.

Your well-being matters.

## How to Respond

Take steps to protect yourself.

- Acknowledge the problem
- Communicate your concerns
- Seek support if needed
You are not alone.

## When to Leave

If the relationship continues to harm you, it may be time to walk away.

Your safety and happiness should come first.

Do not ignore serious warning signs.

## Conclusion

An unhealthy relationship can affect every aspect of your life.

By recognizing the signs, you can take control of your situation.

Healthy relationships are built on respect and care.

You deserve to feel safe and valued.
