A relationship does not become toxic overnight. Toxic patterns usually develop slowly through repeated negative behaviors. What starts as small disagreements can grow into constant conflict, emotional stress, and lack of respect.

Recognizing early signs of toxicity can help prevent long-term emotional damage. Awareness allows you to address unhealthy patterns before they become permanent.

Healthy relationships should bring support, not constant stress.

## Early Signs of a Toxic Relationship

### Constant Criticism

Negative comments about your personality or decisions can damage confidence.

Constructive feedback should never feel like humiliation.

### Lack of Trust

Suspicion and jealousy may become frequent.

Trust is essential for stability.

### Emotional Manipulation

Your partner may use guilt or pressure to control decisions.

This behavior creates imbalance.

### Feeling Drained

Spending time together may leave you emotionally exhausted.

This exhaustion is a major warning sign.

## How to Address Toxic Behavior

- Recognize unhealthy patterns
- Set clear boundaries
- Communicate your concerns
- Seek support from trusted people
- Prioritize your well-being
Ignoring toxic behavior can lead to emotional burnout.

Healthy relationships should feel safe and supportive.
